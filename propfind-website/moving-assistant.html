⚖️ Rental Agreement Guide

What should a rental agreement include? ▼
A valid rental agreement should include: full names of landlord and tenant, property address and description, monthly rent amount, security deposit, duration and renewal terms, notice period, maintenance responsibilities, and rules on subletting and pets.
11-month vs 12-month lease — what's the difference? ▼
An 11-month lease avoids mandatory stamp duty registration (under ₹500 in most states). A 12-month or longer agreement must be registered and is legally stronger. For short-term stays, 11-month agreements are common in India.
How to handle security deposit disputes? ▼
Document the property's condition with photos on move-in. Deductions must be justified (damage beyond normal wear). If disputed, approach the Rent Control Authority or file a complaint in consumer court. Keep all receipts of rent payments.
Tenant rights under Indian law ▼
Under the Model Tenancy Act 2021, tenants have the right to a written agreement, receipt for every payment, advance notice before entry by landlord, and protection from illegal eviction. The security deposit is capped at 2 months' rent for residential properties.

🚪 Move-In Checklist

⚡ Utility Setup

📌 Transfer electricity to your name at the local EB office with your agreement and ID.
💧 Contact your city's water board to update address for water bill.
🌐 Book broadband 1–2 weeks before moving. Top providers: Airtel, BSNL, Hathway, ACT.
⛽ Transfer LPG cylinder connection via Indane / HP / Bharat Gas app or dealer visit.

📬 Address Change Reminders

🪪 Update Aadhaar address at nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre.
💳 Update address on PAN, bank accounts, and credit cards online.
📦 Redirect post at IndiaPost website for up to 12 months.
🏫 Notify employer, schools, and insurance providers of your new address.
🚗 Update vehicle RC address at your regional RTO if moving cities.

🚛 Packers & Movers Tips

📅 Book at least 2 weeks in advance — especially for end-of-month moves.
💰 Get 3 quotes before finalising. Verify GST registration of the company.
📋 Insist on a written inventory of all items packed.
📸 Photo-document fragile items before packing begins.
🔒 Be present during loading and unloading to verify all items.

🌐 Internet & Tech Setup

📶 Check provider coverage in your locality first (JioFiber, Airtel, ACT).
💡 Install smart home or Wi-Fi mesh devices before furniture arrives.
🔌 Test all power sockets and earthing before plugging in appliances.
📡 For DTH / cable, register your new address online or call provider.
